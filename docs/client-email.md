Hi,

Thanks for the call last week. As promised, a bit more about what I need.

I get a plan from a volunteer committee for nearly every event in town: the markets, the runs, the festivals. I have to sign each one off or send it back, and most of my time goes on working out whether it can actually happen by the date they've put down. Committees are always optimistic about dates. They mean well, but usually one or two people do most of the work, and it's never as simple as adding up the weeks.

What I'd like, roughly in order:

- Open a plan and see at a glance whether it will make its date, and if not, how far off it is.
- Know where each remark comes from in the plan. When I send a plan back I quote it to the committee, so it has to be what they actually wrote.
- Sometimes I disagree with a review. It's got a task wrong, or I know something the plan doesn't say. I'd like to correct it and see what that changes.

The last tool we tried gave me a long list of comments and I couldn't tell which ones mattered. I'm not technical, so simple is good.
