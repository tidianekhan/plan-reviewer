# Pellerby Spring Market

Submitted by the Friends of Pellerby Town Hall to the Town Council events office.
Contact: Joan Whitlock, chair of the Friends.

## Summary

The Friends of Pellerby Town Hall would like to run the Spring Market again this year. It is a one-day craft and produce market in the main hall and the side room of the Town Hall, with around thirty stalls, a tea and cake stall run by our members, and a small children's corner with colouring and a book swap.

The whole market is indoors, so there is no road closure and no outdoor equipment to worry about. The Town Hall already has its own fire safety arrangements, a defibrillator in the lobby and a trained first-aider on the caretaking staff, who will be on duty that day.

The market is on a Saturday, 21 days after our kickoff meeting, and everything in the task list below needs to be finished by then.

Three of us share the work, the same as last year: Joan looks after the bookings and the floor plan, Martin looks after the stallholders and the furniture, and Leila looks after the publicity and the volunteers.

## Notes on the tasks

**The hall.** Joan will book the main hall and the side room through the Town Hall office as soon as we have the go-ahead. Both rooms are free on the Saturday we want, and the office has said they can hold the date for us for a few days.

**Stallholders.** Martin has last year's list of stallholders, and most of them have already said they would like to come back. He will write to all of them and to the people on the waiting list, take the pitch fees, and confirm who has which pitch. We are keeping it to around thirty stalls so the aisles stay wide enough for buggies and wheelchairs.

**Tea and cake.** As always, the tea and cake stall is run by our members and the money goes to the Friends. Joan will sort out the urns, the rota of bakers and the float.

**Publicity.** Leila will design an A4 poster and a smaller flyer in the same style as last year's. Once they are printed she will put them up in the library, the shops on the High Street and the surgery, and drop flyers at the two primary schools.

**Floor plan and furniture.** Once the stallholders are confirmed, Joan will draw up the floor plan for both rooms, showing every pitch, the tea and cake stall and the children's corner, and keeping the fire exits clear. The Town Hall has twenty tables of its own, so Martin will hire the extra tables and chairs from the community centre to match the floor plan.

**Volunteers.** Leila will put together the rota for the day: helpers at the door, on the tea and cake stall and in the children's corner, in two-hour shifts. She needs the floor plan first to know how many helpers each area needs.

## On the day

Stallholders can arrive from 8am and the doors open to the public from 10am to 3pm. Entry is free. The caretaker locks up at 5pm, by which time the rooms will be cleared and the tables stacked.

## Money

Pitch fees cover the hire of the hall and the extra furniture, and the printing. Anything made on the tea and cake stall goes towards the Friends' fund for the new hall curtains. We are not asking the Council for a grant this year.
